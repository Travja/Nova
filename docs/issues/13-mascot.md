The astronaut currently drifts on empty states and sign-in and does nothing else.
It could carry the emotional read of the whole app.

**Give it state** driven by the overall picture:

- Everything closed: relaxed, floating, maybe asleep.
- On pace: working — a wrench, a tether adjustment.
- Behind with time left: alert, pointing at the thing that needs attention.
- Period nearly over and well short: drifting away from the ship, tether taut.

Keep it warm rather than scolding. Nothing here should read as guilt; a missed
orbit is information, not a failure.

**Done when**

- The mascot's state derives from real snapshot data, not a random pick.
- Each state is distinguishable in a 160px sprite.
- It is skippable for anyone who finds it distracting (see the preferences issue).
